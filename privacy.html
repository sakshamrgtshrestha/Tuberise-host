Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

TubeRise is a mobile app operated by OPERATOR_NAME_HERE (“we”, “us”). People watch videos and take part in tasks to earn in-app coins, and spend coins to promote their own videos. This policy explains what information we handle and why. Questions: SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE.

Information we collect

  • Google account information: your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID, when you sign in with Google.
  • YouTube information (read only): your YouTube channel ID and handle, and whether you have liked, subscribed to or commented on the specific video in a task you are completing.
  • App activity: your coin balance, the tasks you completed and when, the campaigns you create (video link, channel name, number of views, likes, subscribers or comments requested, cost and progress), and whether your account has been banned.
  • Technical information: our service providers, such as Google Firebase, may automatically process technical data like your IP address to run their services.

How we use Google and YouTube data

When you sign in, TubeRise asks for these Google permissions:

  • Profile and email: to create your account and show your name and photo.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read your channel ID and to check whether you are subscribed to a channel or have commented on a video in a task.
  • Manage your YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl): YouTube requires this broader permission before an app can read whether you have liked a video. TubeRise uses it only to read that like status. It never likes, subscribes, comments, posts, edits or deletes anything on your behalf. Any like, subscribe or comment is made by you, on YouTube.

We use this data only to verify that you completed a task and to award coins. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not transfer it to others except as described below. Your YouTube access tokens stay on your device and are used to contact YouTube directly. We do not store them on our servers.

TubeRise’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TubeRise also uses the YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

How we share information

  • Other TubeRise users. When you create a campaign, its video link, channel name and your account identifier are visible to other signed-in users so they can complete it. Your coin balance and Google profile are not shown to other users.
  • Service providers. We store data with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes it on our behalf.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our users and service from abuse.

We do not sell your personal information. TubeRise does not currently show third-party ads or use third-party analytics tools.

Security

Data is stored in Google Firebase and protected by access rules so users can reach only the data they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How long we keep data, and your choices

We keep your account and activity data while your account is active. To delete your account and data, email us from the address on your account. We will delete it within 30 days of a verified request.

You can also withdraw TubeRise’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, TubeRise can no longer read your YouTube data.

Children

TubeRise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will update the app and this page when how we handle Google data changes.
