TubeRise

Watch videos from other creators and earn coins. Spend your coins to get your own videos watched.

How it works

  1. Watch. Open a task in the app and watch the video for the time shown. You earn the coins listed on the task.
  2. Engage. Some tasks ask you to like, subscribe or comment on the video. You do this yourself, on YouTube.
  3. Spend. Use your coins to create a campaign for your own video and choose how many people should take part.

Each task can be completed once per person. Coins are an in-app reward and cannot be exchanged for cash.

How TubeRise uses your YouTube data

When you sign in with Google, TubeRise asks for access to your YouTube account. It uses that access only to read whether you liked, subscribed to or commented on the video in a task, so it can award your coins.

TubeRise never likes, subscribes, comments or posts on your behalf. Read the privacy policy for full details.
